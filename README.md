## Project Overview

The Enterprise Revenue & Profitability Analytics Suite is an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed using Power BI, SQL, and Google BigQuery to analyze enterprise-wide sales performance, profitability trends, customer behavior, and operational KPIs.

This project focuses on transforming raw transactional data into actionable business insights through interactive dashboards, KPI storytelling, advanced SQL analysis, and scalable data modeling techniques.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor revenue performance, identify profit-driving segments, analyze customer and product trends, and support data-driven business decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Organizations often face challenges in consolidating sales and profitability data across products, customers, and regions. Traditional reporting methods limit visibility into operational performance and delay strategic decision-making.

This project addresses these challenges by building a centralized analytics and reporting solution capable of tracking business KPIs, profitability metrics, customer segmentation, and regional sales performance through dynamic and interactive reporting.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze enterprise sales and profitability performance
- Track key business KPIs through executive dashboards
- Identify high-performing products and customer segments
- Perform regional and category-wise profitability analysis
- Enable drill-through reporting and interactive business exploration
- Support strategic business decision-making using data storytelling

## Key Business Insights

- A small percentage of products contributed to the majority of overall profits, indicating strong Pareto distribution behavior.
- Certain high-revenue regions generated comparatively lower profit margins due to operational inefficiencies.
- Customer segmentation analysis identified high-sales but low-margin customer groups.
- Profit bridge analysis highlighted the primary drivers impacting margin fluctuations across categories.
- Interactive KPI tracking improved visibility into enterprise performance metrics.
